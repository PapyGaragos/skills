# Approach

The approach in its file's layout, as in the Example in SKILL.md, as plain text with no code fence around it (the Example's fence only marks it): frontmatter with `name` and `contributes-to`, the lead line, then `## Steps`, `## Assumptions`, `## Covers` and `## Risks`, with a `### <effort-slug>` heading per effort under the last two. Covers is always there; leave out any other empty section. Nothing before or after it.
